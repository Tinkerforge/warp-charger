# Technical Specification

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Chargers | One or two WARP4 Chargers Smart/Pro, depending on stand version; ordered separately |
| Material | Galvanised steel, 2 mm thick |
| Finish | DB703 powder coating |
| Protection rating | IP54 |
| Dimensions excluding base, cable holders and chargers | Approx. 390.6 × 1402.3 × 154.5 mm (W/H/D) |
| Overall height including base plate | 1408.3 mm |
| Base plate | 435 × 6 × 200 mm (W/H/D) |
| Weight | Approx. 30 kg without chargers |
| Mounting | Four M8 threaded rods in the foundation, secured from inside |
| Rear panel | Split, with two square-key latches per panel; locks optional |
| Distribution enclosure | Optional, with DIN rail, terminals and cable connection kit |
| Optional enclosure internal dimensions | 265 × 370 × 120 mm (W/H/D) |

The drawing distinguishes the housing depth of 152.5 mm from the 154.5 mm depth
including the rear projection. The base plate, cable holders and chargers
increase the space required.

## Maintenance and Cleaning

The stand does not require maintenance. Continue to follow the maintenance
instructions for the installed chargers.

Never clean the stand or chargers with a pressure washer or similar equipment.
Wipe with a damp cloth or a non-aggressive cleaner, following the cleaner
manufacturer's instructions. Do not use aggressive cleaning products, waxes or
solvents. Test cleaning products on an inconspicuous area first.
