# Introduction

The WARP4 Charger Stand is a freestanding mounting solution for one or two
[WARP4 Chargers](/warp4/introduction.md). Smart and Pro variants can be combined
freely. The chargers are ordered separately.

![WARP4 stands with chargers and cable holders installed](/img/warp4_stand/stand_versions.jpg)

The stand is made of 2 mm galvanised steel with a DB703 powder coating. Its IP54
rating makes it suitable for outdoor installation. It is manufactured in Germany.
The supplied preassembled mounting jig positions the threaded rods in the concrete
foundation. The stand is bolted down from inside, and its split rear panel gives
access to the supply cables.

## Variants

- **For one charger:** Mounting position on the front, with a closed upper rear panel.
- **For two chargers:** One mounting position each on the front and rear.

The single-charger version can be converted by replacing the upper rear panel
with a panel containing the openings and mounting points for a second charger.
Both chargers must be fitted on the two-charger version; the second mounting
position must not be left open.

The WARP4 stand is designed exclusively for WARP4 Chargers. For the previous
model, see [WARP Charger Stand](/warp_ladesäule/introduction.md).

## Optional Distribution Enclosure

The stand can be ordered **with or without a distribution enclosure and cable
connection kit**. The optional enclosure is equipped for one or two chargers
with a DIN rail, terminals and a DIN-rail RJ45 coupler. It comes with the matching
power, protective-earth and Ethernet patch cables.

The enclosure also offers space for additional components such as an Ethernet
switch or LTE router. When installing the stand without this kit, suitable
connection and protective-earth cables must be provided separately.

## Access Protection

Each rear panel has two latches, opened with the supplied cabinet key. Remove
the lower panel before removing the upper panel. The latches can optionally be
replaced with locks.
